Quality Assurance

Certified Quality. Every Pile.

Independent testing and full audit-ready documentation — issued in English, referencing GB50007, JGJ106 and BS EN ISO 22477. Complete traceability for every pile: borehole logs, pour records, cage inspection certificates and test results.

  • 01

    Kentledge Static Load Test

    Concrete block reaction verifies pile bearing capacity — the gold standard for pile acceptance, accepted by all Nigerian consultants.

  • 02

    Self-Balancing Test (O-Cell)

    Hydraulic load box tests shaft friction and toe resistance independently — no heavy kentledge, ideal for restricted sites.

  • 03

    Low-Strain Integrity Test (PIT)

    Rapid stress-wave screening of pile shaft continuity — suitable for 100% QA survey.

  • 04

    Cross-Hole Sonic Logging (CSL)

    Ultrasonic pulse between pre-installed tubes pinpoints concrete defects by depth — industry standard for Ø600mm+ piles.
