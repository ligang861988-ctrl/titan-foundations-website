Core Services · Lagos, Nigeria

Foundation Engineering, End to End.

From geotechnical assessment and scheme design to pile construction, ground improvement, deep excavation support and certified testing — delivered to GB and BS EN standards by a team permanently based in Lagos.

01

Pile Foundation Construction

Our core business. Rotary bored piles from Ø600mm to Ø2,400mm and depths to 80m, PHC precast piles installed by static press, and steel sheet piles for retaining and cofferdam works. Several rigs on the ground allow fast mobilisation anywhere in Lagos.

  • Rotary bored piles Ø600–2,400mm, depth to 80m, torque to 220 kN·m
  • PHC precast piles by static press up to 6,000 kN — zero vibration, low noise
  • Steel sheet piles, casing and cofferdam installation
  • Full records: borehole logs, pour records, cage inspection certificates

Typical applications: High-rise towers, industrial plants, bridges and waterfront works

02

Foundation Scheme Design

Optimised foundation solutions derived from your geotechnical data — pile type selection, capacity calculation, layout optimisation and complete drawings. Design and construction can be delivered as one package, which removes interface risk and usually reduces cost.

  • Interpretation of borehole and CPT data
  • Pile type selection, capacity and settlement calculation
  • Pile layout optimisation and shop drawings
  • Design-build delivery under a single contract

Typical applications: Developers, EPC contractors and international consultants

03

Ground Improvement

Lagos sits on soft coastal clays, loose sands and uncontrolled fill. We improve marginal ground so that both shallow and deep foundations perform: high-pressure jet grouting, double-axis and triple-axis cement-soil mixing, and CSM/SMW walls that support the excavation and control seepage at the same time.

  • High-pressure jet grouting (triple fluid), Ø300–1,200mm, depth 30m+
  • Double-axis cement-soil mixing for soft soil treatment
  • Triple-axis CSM / SMW stop-water curtains to 35m
  • Treatment of soft coastal soils and problematic fill

Typical applications: Reclaimed land, Lekki and Victoria Island soft ground, warehouses and estates

04

Deep Excavation Support

Basements and deep pits in Lagos need both a reliable retaining system and proper groundwater control. We design and install the support system, then monitor it throughout construction.

  • Basement retaining walls and soldier / secant pile systems
  • CSM curtains and steel sheet pile walls
  • Strutting, anchors and dewatering
  • Continuous monitoring: settlement, deflection and groundwater

Typical applications: Basement car parks, commercial podiums and underground works on congested sites

05

Pile Testing & Inspection

Independent verification that every pile performs as designed. All reports are issued in English and reference GB50007, JGJ106 and BS EN ISO 22477, so they are accepted by Lagos consultants, structural engineers and lenders.

  • Kentledge static load test with full load-settlement curves
  • Self-balancing O-Cell test, up to 10,000+ kN
  • Low-strain PIT and high-strain PDA dynamic testing
  • Cross-hole sonic logging (CSL) and core drilling

Typical applications: Pile acceptance testing, QA/QC programmes and handover documentation

06

Equipment Leasing

Need piling capacity without buying machines? We lease our rigs and specialised equipment with experienced operators and maintenance support included, mobilised to your site anywhere in Lagos.

  • SUNWARD SWDM220-II rotary drilling rigs with operators
  • Jet grouting machines and mixing rigs
  • Static pile press, bored pile drills and support equipment
  • Maintenance and spare parts support included

Typical applications: Contractors with short-term or seasonal piling requirements
